About the book

The Lens Adjusted is an evocative collection of romantic and spiritual poetry.

Exploring life, love and faith through a fresh perspective. Written in a graceful, traditional style, the poems invite us to pause and reflect on the things that shape our lives. Danny Boatfield’s playful and imaginative artwork complements the poetry and brings its ideas to life.

Beautifully presented in hardback, the book features 22 full-page colour illustrations, alongside smaller black-and-white illustrations throughout. Finished with gold foil detailing, The Lens Adjusted has been created as a book to be treasured and revisited.

The book is currently available as a limited first edition.
