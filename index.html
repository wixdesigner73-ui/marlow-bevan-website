About Marlow

Musician, producer and poet.

Marlow Bevan is a musician, producer and poet, originally from Warwick, England. He studied music at the Liverpool Institute for Performing Arts, established by Sir Paul McCartney.

After relocating to Los Angeles, he formed The Green Children (TGC) with Norwegian singer Milla Alice. The electropop duo went on to sign with Universal Music Group, with their work receiving international attention from publications including Rolling Stone, Music Week and The Huffington Post.

Alongside his work in music, poetry has remained a constant part of Marlow’s writing. His poems explore themes of love, faith and everyday life.

The Lens Adjusted, his first poetry collection, brings together this work alongside illustrations by Danny Boatfield.
